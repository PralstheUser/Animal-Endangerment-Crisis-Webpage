Why Does This Matter?

Our planet's biodiversity is in crisis. Over the past few decades, we've witnessed an unprecedented decline in animal populations worldwide. Climate change, habitat destruction, poaching, and pollution are driving countless species toward extinction.

This educational resource aims to raise awareness about the endangered animals facing critical threats and inspire action towards conservation efforts. Every species plays a vital role in our ecosystem, and their survival depends on our immediate and committed efforts.

🐯 Bengal Tiger

Once roaming across Asia, only 3,900 tigers remain in the wild. Habitat loss and poaching are the primary threats to their survival.

🐘 African Elephant

Poaching for ivory tusks has devastated populations. Around 20,000 elephants are killed annually for their tusks.

🐢 Leatherback Turtle

These ancient mariners are dying from plastic pollution and fishing nets. Ocean warming also threatens their survival.

🦁 African Lion

Lion populations have declined by 94% over the past century due to habitat loss and human-wildlife conflict.

🐼 Giant Panda

Habitat fragmentation in Chinese forests has isolated populations. Conservation efforts have improved their status slightly.

🐋 Blue Whale

Once hunted to near extinction, blue whales face threats from ocean noise pollution and changing food sources.
